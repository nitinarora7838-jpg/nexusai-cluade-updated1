FAQ

Questions before you book.

Will callers know they're talking to AI?

The voices sound natural and respond in under a second, so most conversations feel normal. We recommend the agent says it's an AI assistant at the start. Callers can ask for a person at any time.

Can it use our existing phone number?

Yes. We forward your current number to the agent for all calls, only after-hours, or only when your team doesn't pick up. You choose.

Which languages does it speak?

English plus many other languages, and it can switch mid-call when the caller does. We test the voice and accent with you before launch.

What happens when the AI can't help?

It transfers the call to the right person, or takes a message and alerts your team on WhatsApp with a summary. You decide which cases always go to a human.

How much does it cost?

A one-time setup fee plus a monthly fee based on call minutes. Because every business has different call volumes, you get a fixed quote after the free audit, before you commit to anything.

Are calls recorded? What about privacy?

Calls can be recorded and transcribed for quality, with a consent message at the start. Data stays in your accounts, and we sign an NDA and a data processing agreement.

Can I talk to someone first?

Yes. Email ai@nexus-aisolution.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 70112 25289.
